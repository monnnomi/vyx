Privacy & data

VYX early access · Updated November 9, 2026

Signing in with X

VYX uses X to confirm your identity. We save your X account ID, username, display name and profile image URL. We do not request your email, post to X, read X direct messages or retain a token for ordinary sign-in. If you separately enable task verification, the short-lived read-only token is encrypted on the server as described below.

Your VYX activity

Your VYX profile, posts, comments, reactions, follows and season leaderboard are public. Saved posts, your chest inventory and reward history belong to your account. Direct messages are accessible through the app only to the two participants. They are stored in the database and are not end-to-end encrypted; the service operator can access stored data.

Message views

When a signed-in member sees a message on screen, VYX records their account ID, the message ID and the first view time. Each account counts once per message; senders do not count their own messages. Message participants see an aggregate unique-view count, not a list of viewers. Your own view acknowledgement helps prevent duplicate requests. Views are recorded separately from VP and do not earn points. Counts begin with this feature and do not reconstruct past views.

Rooms

Room names, descriptions, owners and member counts are publicly discoverable. Any signed-in X account can join an open room. Only current members can read the room history, see its member list or send messages through the app. Joining gives access to existing history. Room messages are stored in the database and are not end-to-end encrypted. Room owners can remove messages and close their rooms. Leaving removes your access but does not erase messages you already sent; removed messages and closed rooms remain stored for the operator.

X task verification

Optional task verification requests read-only access to your X profile, follows, liked posts and posts. VYX uses that access only to check the published tasks. It does not follow, like, repost, comment or publish for you. The access token is encrypted at rest and usable for at most two hours; no refresh token is requested. You can disconnect verification from Quests. Expired credentials are removed when you next open Quests or during cleanup. We retain task IDs, verification times, evidence post/account IDs and VP receipts to prevent duplicate rewards. Full liked-post lists and timelines are not stored. Task rewards come from the promotion reserve, and can affect your leaderboard and NFT eligibility.

NFT whitelist

Members who currently qualify for a free NFT or a 50% discount appear in the public whitelist with their VYX profile and qualification tier. Lists update automatically from confirmed lifetime VP and registered referral totals. Detailed eligibility progress, exact deficits and the list of invited accounts remain private to the account owner and VYX administrators. The whitelist shows current eligibility and does not open NFT minting.

Room farming

During farming seasons, the server records eligible room-message contributions, compact fingerprints of normalized text to reject repeats and similar messages, timestamps, qualifying UTC activity days, per-round coefficients and four-hour payout receipts. These records determine your VP share. Your eligible activity is visible to room members; the authorized owner dashboard shows payout records and budget totals. Deleted messages lose farming activity only before their window closes. Closed payout receipts remain stored. Wallet linking and NFT ownership verification are planned and are not enabled in this version.

Photos and videos

Attachments are stored in a private Vercel Blob store. Photos and videos attached to public posts or comments can be viewed by visitors. Room attachments require current membership in that room and are hidden when the message is removed or the room closes. Direct attachments are available only to the two participants. They are not end-to-end encrypted; the operator and hosting provider can access stored files. File size, type, owner, upload time and its post or conversation association are recorded. Uploads that are not sent expire after 24 hours and are removed by background cleanup. Published attachments remain stored with their messages. Original image or video metadata may remain in uploaded files.

Cookies and storage

A necessary, secure sign-in cookie lasts up to 30 days. A short-lived cookie protects the X sign-in flow. Only a hashed copy of the session token is stored in the database. Your visual theme, animation and drop sound preferences are saved in this browser. Old prototype points are not imported into real accounts.

Activity and mint records

VYX stores connection times and collectible mint receipts. A necessary one-day presence cookie helps count active browsers. Visible pages update their activity about every 30 seconds; the owner dashboard counts activity within the last 90 seconds, separates signed-in members from guest browsers and shows connection and mint records only to the authorized administrator. Presence tokens are stored only as hashes.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the application, Neon stores community data and X handles sign-in. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and profile images may load from X. These providers receive the connection information needed to deliver their services. VYX stores a keyed hash of network addresses for rate limits, rather than a raw address in its rate-limit table. Hosting providers may maintain their own request logs.

Optional analytics

With your permission, VYX uses Google Analytics, Yandex Metrica and Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits and navigation between named app sections. Google and Yandex may store analytics cookies and process technical connection information, including your network address, browser and device. Vercel Web Analytics uses a cookie-free visitor measurement method. VYX sends section names instead of room, person, post or receipt identifiers, and removes query parameters from measured page addresses. Message text and profile details are not sent by our analytics integration. Session replay, form tracking, automatic link tracking and advertising personalization are disabled in this integration.

You can decline analytics and use VYX normally. Your choice is saved in this browser; change it in Settings → Analytics. Declining stops future analytics collection by this integration, but does not delete previously collected statistics. Local development, preview deployments, the admin dashboard and X sign-in endpoints do not load these trackers.

Retention and controls

Community content, messages and reward records remain stored until removed by the operator. Signing out revokes your current session. You can edit your public profile and revoke VYX access in your X account settings. Revoking access on X does not delete existing VYX data. A self-service account deletion flow is not yet available in this early version.

Points

VP are application points. They are not tokens, deposits or a promise of monetary value. Future token terms and eligibility are not finalized.

Member controls, reports and deletion requests

Settings lets you choose notification categories and review muted or blocked members and rooms. Reports are stored with the reported item reference, reason and optional explanation so the VYX administrator can review them. Reporting a private message makes that reported message available to the administrator for review.

To request account deletion, sign in and open Settings → Request account deletion. The request is sent to the administrator; it does not erase your data immediately. Its status appears in Settings. Existing deletion contact options on this page remain available if you cannot sign in.

Product improvement measurements

With analytics allowed, VYX records named actions such as search, opening a room and publishing, with a section and success/empty/error outcome. These records contain no user ID, search query or message text and expire after 30 days. The administrator also sees aggregate counts of reply exchanges derived from existing room records. These counts do not prove conversation quality or measure individual retention. With analytics allowed, additional named signals measure visits, sign-in, first room joins and messages, the first accepted VP contribution, the first confirmed reward seen and a next-day return. Browser-local markers avoid repeating first-action signals and remember the initial visit day; account markers are not transmitted to analytics. Incoming traffic is classified only as X, Telegram, direct or other, without forwarding invitation codes, full referrer addresses or query parameters. These consented browser signals are not cross-device retention statistics.

Invitations

When you open an invitation link, this browser remembers the latest inviter for up to 30 days. On your first successful X registration, VYX records who invited you. Each new account has at most one inviter. Referral totals appear in the public leaderboard; the list of invited accounts is not published by this feature. Existing accounts and self-invitations do not count.
